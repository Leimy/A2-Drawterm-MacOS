RadioGlobe station catalogue snapshots

RadioGlobeStations.txt: 9,297 entries copied without conversion from stations2.
RadioGlobeStationsTop.txt: 899 entries copied without conversion from stations.
Source: David Leimbach's local gcc-9front/realworld/radioglobe project.
The files retain mkstations' provenance comments: generated from the open
radio-browser.info community database. Names, coordinates and stream URLs are
original data, not generated by the assistant.

Format: latitude longitude "station name" http-or-https-url
Duplicate coordinates are intentional; use the Stations search window to
choose among stations sharing a marker. Format labels are hints from names
and URLs; the audio decoder determines the actual stream codec.

The larger catalogue is the default. To use the smaller one, run:
WMRadioGlobe.Open RadioGlobeStationsTop.txt

To provide your own list, place RadioGlobeStations.txt in the A2 launch
directory, or pass a filename to WMRadioGlobe.Open. These are snapshots:
stations may change URLs or go offline. Play failures appear in the globe.
